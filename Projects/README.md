Asteroid README

Summary:
This game has the player control a spaceship. Similar to the altered version of the Timber game, objects will be flying towards the player in the form of asteroids. These asteroids will come in 1 of three lanes, Top, Middle or Right. The player can use the arrow keys to move their ship up a lane or down a lane to dodge the asteroids. As time goes on, Score will be accumulated and the asteroids will be flying faster and faster. The goal of the game is to survive as long as possible without having an asteroid hit your ship.

Special Ability:
The special ability in the game comes in the form of slowing down time. If the player lives for 10 seconds, a bar at the bottom of the screen will become full. This gives the player the ability to slow down time by holding space to make dodging asteroids easier. A slight liberty from the assignment is the fact that this ability is a resource. If the player only holds space for a moment, only a little bit of the resource bar will go down. However, the player may only activate the ability if the bar is fully charged. This means, the longer you use the ability, the longer you must wait for it to come back. This incentivizes players to use their ability more sparingly and introduces a new level of skill to the game.

Additional Modification:
My additional modification is the fact that my game has three lanes. This creates more in depth dodging because now the game has become more than "be in the opposite lane of the obstacle". On top of that, the changes to the ability are my second modification.
